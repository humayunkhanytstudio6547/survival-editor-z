## 🧪 Core Forging Capabilities

### 🩸 Vitality Matrix Engineering
Adjust the **metabolic essence** of your survivor—not just health and thirst bars, but the **underlying recovery rates**, **max capacity thresholds**, and **sickness progression curves**. Forge a character who's a tank, a glass cannon, or a fragile scholar who thrives on knowledge, not bullets.

### 🎒 Inventory Alchemy
Transform your pack into a **portable museum of curated possibilities**. Duplicate rare schematics, re-balance ammunition stacks, or **teleport specific tool tiers** into your hands for testing. This isn't about giving yourself everything—it's about **authoring loadouts for specific theatrical performances** in your world.

### 🌡️ Environmental Resistance Calibration
Adjust your **exposure to the elements**—heat, cold, radiation, and the dreaded **infection curve**. Create a character who's a desert-dwelling lizard or a tundra-born polar bear. This unlocks **entirely new playstyles** based on biome-specialization instead of gear-specialization.

### ⏳ Time-Scale Remapping
The game's internal clock is your **narrative metronome**. Slow down day/night cycles for extended exploration, or speed up time for **rapid-cycle testing** of crop growth, forge output, and material decay. You're not time-traveling; you're **conducting a temporal orchestra**.

### 🧠 Experience & Skill-Curve Reshaping
Redefine how your survivor learns. Adjust **XP multipliers for specific actions** (stealth kills vs. mining), re-balance skill point costs, or **lock certain perk tiers** to force creative problem-solving. This is **pacing control** for your personal story arc.

## 🎛️ The Forge Interface: A Sculptor's Workbench

Forget command-line jargon or cryptic hex editors. Genome Forge presents a **visual, node-based workbench** that reads your live game state like a **biological chart**. 

- **Live State Reflector:** A dashboard that mirrors health, stamina, inventory weight, and core stats in real-time.
- **Scenario Pre-Sets:** Browse a library of **curated challenges**—"Starving Artist" (zero food, max crafting skill), "The Glass Nomad" (max speed, min defense), "Veteran's Despair" (max skills, desert biome start).

- **Mutation Log:** Every adjustment is logged, so you can **reverse-engineer your own gameplay philosophy** or share your forge sequence with the community.

## 🚀 Getting Started Guide

1. **Initialization:** Launch the game and enter a single-player world. Once loaded, run the Genome Forge executable from your desktop.
2. **Synchronization:** The tool auto-detects the game process and establishes a **non-invasive read-write handshake**. No external drivers required.
3. **Sculpting:** Use the dashboard to adjust variables. The game will reflect changes within 2-3 real-time seconds.
4. **Save & Sequester:** Your changes are applied to the active session. We recommend creating a **manual save** before major forges to act as a **fallback fossil**.

## 🧰 Troubleshooting & Optimization

- **Lag or Frame Drop:** Adjust the "Reflection Rate" slider in Settings to reduce how often the tool reads game state.
- **Detected as False Positive:** Add the executable to your antivirus exclusions. The forge uses **memory mapping techniques** similar to legitimate benchmarking tools; this can occasionally trigger heuristic scanners.
- **Game Update Incompatibility:** Wait 12-24 hours after a major alpha patch for our team to release a **compatibility patch** that updates the state parser.
